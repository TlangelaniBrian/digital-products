# The SA Property Investor AI Pack
## 36 ChatGPT and Claude Prompts for South African Property Investors

**Launch price:** R249  
**Regular price:** R399

## What this is

This pack is for South African property investors who are tired of staring at deals, WhatsApps, lease admin, maintenance chaos, and half-finished spreadsheets.

It gives you 36 practical prompts you can paste into ChatGPT or Claude to move faster on:

- deal screening
- bond and cash-flow thinking
- tenant communication
- maintenance planning
- rental operations
- exit and portfolio decisions

This is not legal or financial advice. It is an operator pack to help you think clearly, draft faster, and avoid sloppy admin.

---

## How to use this pack

For best results, always give the model:

- the property type
- the suburb and city
- the purchase price
- expected rent
- known costs
- your target return
- any constraints or risks

Replace bracketed placeholders like `[PURCHASE_PRICE]` with your own numbers.

## 1. Deal Screening

### 1. Quick deal screen

> Act as a disciplined South African property investment analyst. I am looking at a [PROPERTY_TYPE] in [SUBURB, CITY] for [PURCHASE_PRICE]. Expected gross monthly rent is [RENT]. Monthly rates, levy, insurance, and other fixed costs are [MONTHLY_COSTS]. I want a fast first-pass verdict. Calculate gross yield, rough net cash flow before tax, biggest deal risks, and whether this deserves deeper due diligence. End with one of three verdicts: kill, watch, or pursue.

### 2. Compare two deals

> Compare these two South African property deals as if you were choosing one for a cash-flow-focused investor. Deal A: [DETAILS]. Deal B: [DETAILS]. Evaluate gross yield, likely net yield, financing risk, tenant-demand risk, maintenance exposure, and resale upside. Recommend the stronger deal and explain why in plain English.

### 3. Area sanity check

> I am considering buying in [SUBURB, CITY]. Give me a property investor sanity-check framework for this area. Focus on tenant demand, likely vacancy risk, affordability of the target tenant, transport access, crime concerns, school/employment anchors, oversupply risk, and what I should verify before buying.

### 4. Offer price backsolve

> I need to backsolve the maximum purchase price for a South African rental property. Desired gross monthly rent is [RENT]. My target gross yield is [TARGET_YIELD]% and my buffer for repairs and vacancy is [BUFFER]%. Calculate the maximum purchase price I should pay, then show the numbers for 3 slightly lower offer levels so I can negotiate properly.

### 5. Flip vs hold decision

> Evaluate whether this South African property should be treated as a flip, a long-term rental, or a pass. Property details: [DETAILS]. Renovation estimate: [RENOVATION_COST]. Likely resale value after upgrade: [ARV]. Likely rent after upgrade: [RENT]. Compare the economics and risk of both strategies and recommend one.

### 6. Red-flag checklist before viewing

> Build me a pre-viewing red-flag checklist for a South African investment property. I want the list grouped into title/legal, structure, security, utilities, tenantability, sectional-title risks if relevant, and neighbourhood warning signs.

## 2. Finance and Cash Flow

### 7. Bond repayment stress test

> Using South African property finance assumptions, stress-test this rental deal. Purchase price: [PURCHASE_PRICE]. Deposit: [DEPOSIT]. Interest rate: [RATE]. Loan term: [TERM]. Rent: [RENT]. Monthly non-bond costs: [COSTS]. Show base case, interest-rate-up case, 1-month vacancy case, and repair-shock case. Tell me how fragile the deal is.

### 8. Full monthly cash-flow draft

> Build a clean monthly rental cash-flow model for this South African property using these inputs: [INPUTS]. Include rent, vacancy allowance, levies, rates, insurance, maintenance reserve, agent fee if any, bond payment, and net cash flow. Also identify which assumptions matter most.

### 9. Deposit decision

> Should I put down a larger deposit on this South African buy-to-let or keep more cash for reserves and the next deal? Property details: [DETAILS]. Available cash: [CASH]. Compare three scenarios: low deposit, medium deposit, and aggressive deposit. Focus on cash flow, risk, and optionality.

### 10. Emergency reserve target

> Based on this property portfolio: [DETAILS], calculate a sensible emergency reserve target for a South African rental investor. Explain the minimum, better, and comfortable reserve levels and what each one protects me against.

### 11. Rent increase timing

> Help me decide whether to increase rent on this property now. Current rent: [RENT]. Market estimate: [MARKET_RENT]. Lease renewal date: [DATE]. Tenant quality: [TENANT_CONTEXT]. Weigh the gain from an increase against vacancy and relationship risk, then suggest a practical number and message angle.

### 12. Buy in personal name vs company

> Give me a practical decision framework for whether to hold this South African rental property in my personal name or inside a company. Do not pretend there is one universal answer. Compare admin burden, tax considerations to verify with a professional, financing implications, estate-planning logic, and scaling consequences.

## 3. Due Diligence and Acquisition

### 13. Document request list

> Draft a due-diligence document request list for a South African property purchase. I want it grouped into seller documents, municipal/levy items, lease/tenant items, plans/compliance items, and finance-related documents. Keep it practical and investor-focused.

### 14. Questions for the estate agent

> Write a sharp list of questions I should send an estate agent about this investment property in South Africa. Property details: [DETAILS]. I want questions that surface motivation, hidden defects, vacancy history, utility problems, body corporate issues, tenant quality, and pricing flexibility.

### 15. Sectional-title risk check

> I am looking at a sectional-title investment property in South Africa. Create a due-diligence checklist focused on body corporate finances, special levies, conduct rules, maintenance backlogs, reserve fund risk, security issues, and anything that can destroy my yield.

### 16. Tenant-in-place assessment

> Assess whether buying a South African rental property with an existing tenant in place is attractive in this case. Details: [DETAILS]. Evaluate payment reliability, rent vs market, lease strength, deposit handling, inspection condition, eviction risk if needed, and the likely hassle factor after transfer.

### 17. Negotiation strategy

> Build a negotiation plan for this South African property deal. Asking price: [ASKING_PRICE]. My ideal price: [IDEAL_PRICE]. Max price: [MAX_PRICE]. Known weaknesses: [WEAKNESSES]. Give me a first offer, fallback positions, leverage points, and when to walk away.

### 18. Renovation scope filter

> Help me separate cosmetic upgrades from dangerous money pits on this South African property. Known issues: [ISSUES]. Classify each item as cosmetic, necessary before tenanting, compliance/safety, or walk-away risk. Then rank them by urgency and expected return.

## 4. Tenant Operations

### 19. Tenant screening rubric

> Create a tenant-screening rubric for a South African landlord. I want a scorecard that covers affordability, employment stability, references, payment behaviour, household composition, risk signals, and what would trigger an automatic decline.

### 20. WhatsApp response to a rental enquiry

> Draft a warm but efficient WhatsApp reply to a prospective tenant asking about this property: [DETAILS]. Ask for the right screening information without sounding hostile or desperate. Keep it concise and professional.

### 21. Viewing-day checklist

> Build a landlord viewing-day checklist for a South African rental property. Include presentation, safety, documents to carry, questions to ask prospects, and what to note immediately after each viewing so I can compare applicants properly.

### 22. Lease-summary explainer

> Draft a plain-English lease summary I can send to a prospective tenant before signing. It should explain rent, deposit, due date, utilities, maintenance responsibilities, notice rules, inspection process, and house rules clearly and professionally.

### 23. Rent arrears first message

> Write a firm but calm first arrears message for a South African tenant who has missed rent. Rent due date was [DATE]. Amount outstanding is [AMOUNT]. I want the tone to be respectful, clear, and legally careful without sounding weak.

### 24. Payment arrangement template

> Draft a payment-arrangement proposal message for a tenant in South Africa who is behind by [AMOUNT]. Include the acknowledgement of arrears, the exact proposed repayment structure, consequences of default, and a polite request for written confirmation.

## 5. Maintenance and Property Management

### 25. Maintenance triage

> Compare these contractor quotes for a South African rental property repair: [QUOTE_A], [QUOTE_B], [QUOTE_C]. Evaluate completeness, hidden omissions, pricing logic, warranty risk, and which contractor looks safest rather than just cheapest.

### 27. Annual maintenance reserve planner

> Build an annual maintenance reserve plan for this rental property in South Africa. Property type: [TYPE]. Age: [AGE]. Known issues: [KNOWN_ISSUES]. I want a practical reserve estimate, likely problem categories, and a suggested monthly reserve amount.

### 28. Tenant maintenance-response message

> Draft a tenant update message about a maintenance issue at my South African rental property. Issue: [ISSUE]. Current status: [STATUS]. Expected next step: [NEXT_STEP]. Make it clear, reassuring, and specific so the tenant does not feel ignored.

### 29. Scope of work draft

> Turn this messy repair brief into a clean contractor scope of work for a South African rental property: [BRIEF]. I want deliverables, exclusions, materials assumptions, start and end expectations, and what evidence of completion I should request.

### 30. Handover inspection list

> Create a move-in or move-out inspection checklist for a South African rental property. Group items by room, utilities, keys/security, appliances, and damage capture. I want it in a format that can be used during an in-person inspection.

## 6. Portfolio and Exit Decisions

### 31. Keep, sell, or refinance

> Help me decide whether to keep, sell, or refinance this South African rental property. Property details: [DETAILS]. Current loan balance: [BALANCE]. Current rent: [RENT]. Condition: [CONDITION]. Alternative use of capital: [ALTERNATIVE]. Compare the three paths and recommend one.

### 32. Portfolio weak-link review

> Review this rental portfolio like a ruthless operator, not a sentimental owner: [PORTFOLIO_DETAILS]. Identify the weakest property, the strongest property, where hidden concentration risk sits, and what I should fix, sell, or double down on.

### 33. Expansion readiness check

> Am I actually ready to buy the next South African investment property? Current portfolio and finances: [DETAILS]. Assess cash reserves, management capacity, debt stress, concentration risk, and whether buying again now is disciplined or stupid.

### 34. Exit price target

> Help me set a realistic exit price strategy for this South African property. Purchase price was [PURCHASE_PRICE]. Current estimated value is [VALUE]. Rent and condition context: [DETAILS]. I want a base-case, optimistic, and fast-sale pricing view, plus what prep work would improve the outcome.

### 35. Investor update message

> Draft a monthly update I can send to a co-investor or partner on a South African property project. Include occupancy, rent collected, maintenance work, current risks, cash position, and next actions. Keep it crisp and professional.

### 36. Weekly operator review

> Act as my property operations reviewer. Based on this week’s events across my South African rentals [DETAILS], tell me: 1) what needs attention now, 2) what is drifting, 3) what admin should be automated or templated, and 4) the single highest-leverage action for next week.
