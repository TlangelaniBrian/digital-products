## Full description
Most founders are not short on ideas. They are short on structure.

AI makes that worse if you use it badly. You end up with scattered prompts, half-finished docs, messy task lists, and a hundred false starts.

**Founder OS** fixes that.

It is a practical operating system for solo builders and small teams who use Claude, ChatGPT, and coding agents to execute. Instead of giving you another lazy prompt pack, it gives you a clean workflow for running product work week after week.

Use it to:
- turn rough ideas into clear PRDs
- break features into buildable tasks
- delegate work to AI agents properly
- triage bugs without noise
- run a weekly founder review
- keep decisions, launches, and post-mortems organised

This is for founders who are building in public, building after-hours, building alone, or building with a very small team and who need more output with less mess.
