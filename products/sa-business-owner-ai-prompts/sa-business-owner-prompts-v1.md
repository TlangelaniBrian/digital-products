# SA Business Owner AI Prompt Pack

Version 1
Price target: R199

Use these prompts with ChatGPT or Claude. Replace bracketed placeholders with your details. Keep the tone plain, specific, and South Africa-aware.

## 1. Quote Request Reply

Prompt:
Write a warm, professional reply to this customer enquiry and turn it into a quotation-ready response.

Offer: [product or service]

Requirements:
- Use South African English
- Keep it concise
- Confirm scope clearly
- End with a direct next step

## How To Use This Pack

- Start with the prompt closest to the task.
- Paste the real business context before sending.
- If the first draft is weak, ask the model to make it shorter, firmer, or more commercial.
- Save the best outputs into your own templates so the pack compounds over time.
